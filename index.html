⚙️ Description of Decisions per Inspected Component Action Protocols
Decision Code Engineering Operational Definition
Reuse A part will be reused or reworked by hand
Regrind By regrinding the teeth, this part can be reused again
Rework By reworking (surfaces besides the teeth), this part can be reused again
Replace by Upgrade Part will be replaced by an upgraded part (independent of the current damage)
Rework to Upgrade Part needs to be reworked to the latest upgrade
Replace by Oversize Current part is scrap and will be replaced by a new (oversized) part
Oversize Part will be reworked to an oversize bore
Flip Part will be reworked so the unloaded flank will become the loaded flank
Scrap Part is not reused and will be replaced by a new part
Scrap(Rework Gear) Integrated planet bearings (roller elements and inner ring) are integrated with the planet wheels.
If the teeth of the planet wheels need to be reworked or reground, then the decision for the planet bearings is "scrap (rework gear)".
In all other situations, e.g. planet wheels are "scrap" or "reused", then the planet bearings are "scrap".
std replacement part Smaller parts will be replaced without inspection, independent of the damage. Example: sealings, bolts, nuts...
